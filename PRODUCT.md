# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Arabic-speaking readers in Musandam, Oman and the wider Gulf region. They check the site on mobile throughout the day for local news — government announcements, development projects, community events, and Musandam city-specific news (Khasab, Deba, Bukha, Madha). Many are Omani nationals with strong regional identity.

## Product Purpose

Musandam.net is the primary Arabic-language news source for Musandam Governorate, Oman. Migrated from a 15-year-old vBulletin forum to a modern Next.js site. Success means readers trust it as their first stop for local news, share articles on WhatsApp and social media, and return daily.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, local, vibrant. The brand is rooted in Musandam's identity — the sea, the mountains, the Omani heritage — expressed through a bold color palette (teal, yellow, green, pink) drawn from the logo's multicolor dot pattern.

## Anti-references

- Generic Arabic news portals that look like 2010-era layouts (Al-Jazeera clones, grey Bootstrap grids)
- Overly corporate / government-feel designs (navy, formal, cold)
- Western news sites force-fitted with RTL — the design must feel natively Arabic

## Design Principles

1. **RTL-native first** — every layout, motion, and reading pattern assumes right-to-left.
2. **Community over broadcast** — warm, approachable tone; not distant media authority.
3. **Color as identity** — the multicolor logo palette is the visual fingerprint; use it boldly.
4. **Mobile-first credibility** — most readers are on mobile; performance and legibility win over decoration.
5. **Motion with purpose** — animate to guide attention and reward interaction, not to impress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Arabic text must be legible at all sizes (min 14px body). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. High contrast for outdoor mobile reading.
